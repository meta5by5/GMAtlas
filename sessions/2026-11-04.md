## Composer button
The Composer link is really the Advisor position's button, labeled with whatever fills that position. It was toggling the hidden side panel, and only Party had the earlier fix.
- **Any panel in that position:** the button now brings its tab to the front and activates it, as Party does. I tested Composer, Navigator, Advisor and Campaign.
- **No tabs open on desktop:** there's no tab to activate, so the button still opens and closes the side panel.
- **Anchored panels:** their nav buttons now go to the Storyboard card instead of opening a placeholder tab.
- **Highlight:** the button now lights up when active, like the other nav buttons.

## Fantasy content
I rewrote 516 oracle entries across `tables-fantasy-full.js` and `tables-dnd5e.js`, and the sci-fi and cyberpunk tables are untouched. Examples:
- "shell company" is now "front guild".
- "company physician" is now "guild physician".
- "a board observer…" is now "a guild council observer counts the dead as lost coin".

Much of the fantasy content was reskinned sci-fi, so I also replaced space and tech leftovers like "air recycler", "radiation" and "autopilot", and "the crew" became "the party". A new test fails if corporate or space terms come back.

Keeping the genres separate also exposed bugs and shared labels that needed their own fantasy versions:
- **Wrong genre on some generators:** Continue Story, Roll Faction Asset, Generate NPC, Generate Mission and a few others could roll sci-fi tables in a fantasy campaign. They now use the campaign's active genre. This was probably one source of the corporate text.
- **Faction rolls:** they pointed at `Corporate Powers`, which the fantasy packs don't have. They now use `Noble Houses & Guilds` and `Faction Turns`, and D&D 5e has its own copies of those tables.
- **Lenses:** each fantasy pack now has its own lens-to-table mapping. Five lenses have new names under fantasy: Technology → Craft, Biology → Nature, Physics → Arcana, Science → Scholarship and Engineering → Artifice.
- **D&D 5e oracle list:** it now uses the fantasy section headings instead of "Space Operations".
