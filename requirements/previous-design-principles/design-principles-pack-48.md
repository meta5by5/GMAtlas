# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 48 --- Ultimate Design Axioms & Closing Principles

## Purpose

This final volume captures the non-negotiable principles that define
Saga Atlas regardless of future technology, framework, or
implementation.

## The Twelve Axioms

1.  The campaign is the product.
2.  Story always comes before mechanics.
3.  Context is more valuable than configuration.
4.  Relationships are more valuable than records.
5.  Mission Control is the application.
6.  Drawers replace navigation whenever practical.
7.  Engines own behavior; UI owns presentation.
8.  Every recommendation must be explainable.
9.  Persistence is sacred.
10. Frictionless Empowerment is the primary UX principle.
11. Extensions must strengthen---not fragment---the platform.
12. The Game Master always has final authority.

## The Golden Workflow

Observe

↓

Understand

↓

Decide

↓

Act

↓

Resolve

↓

Remember

↓

Continue Story

Every subsystem should support this loop.

## The Ultimate Test

Before adding any feature, ask:

-   Does it help tell a better story?
-   Does it reduce cognitive effort?
-   Does it preserve campaign continuity?
-   Does it fit naturally into Mission Control?
-   Will it still make sense years from now?

If not, redesign it.

## Final Vision

Saga Atlas should become:

-   the memory of the campaign,
-   the navigator of context,
-   the organizer of knowledge,
-   the coordinator of gameplay,
-   the assistant that quietly disappears while the story takes center
    stage.

## Closing

The greatest compliment Saga Atlas can receive is:

*"I forgot I was using software. I was simply running an amazing
campaign."*
