# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 46 --- Product Evolution Charter & Enduring Vision

## Purpose

This charter establishes the enduring vision that should guide Saga
Atlas regardless of technology, programming language, or user interface.

## Enduring Mission

Saga Atlas exists to help Game Masters run richer campaigns with less
effort while preserving complete creative ownership.

Every architectural decision should reinforce that mission.

## Vision Statements

### Remember Everything

The application should become the campaign's memory, never forcing the
GM to reconstruct forgotten context.

### Surface What Matters

Rather than exposing every option, Saga Atlas should present the
information and actions most relevant to the current moment.

### Stay Out of the Way

The best interface is the one that quietly supports play without
becoming the focus of attention.

### Grow Without Fragmenting

Future capabilities should extend the platform through engines,
templates, and plugins---not by creating disconnected workflows.

## Product Commitments

Saga Atlas should always strive to be:

-   Story-first
-   Context-aware
-   Extensible
-   Reliable
-   Explainable
-   Portable
-   Offline-capable
-   Upgrade-safe

## Decision Compass

When uncertain, choose the option that:

1.  Preserves campaign continuity.
2.  Reduces cognitive effort.
3.  Improves discoverability.
4.  Reuses existing architecture.
5.  Strengthens Mission Control.
6.  Keeps the Game Master in control.

## Legacy

A successful Saga Atlas campaign should still be usable years later,
even after many software versions.

Campaigns should outlive releases.

## Final Charter

Technology will change.

Frameworks will change.

Rules systems will change.

The mission should not.

Saga Atlas exists so Game Masters can spend less time managing software
and more time creating unforgettable stories together.
