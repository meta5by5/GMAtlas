# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 30 --- Vision 2.0: The Campaign Operating System

## Purpose

This document captures the long-term vision beyond the current
implementation.

Saga Atlas is intended to become the operating system for tabletop
roleplaying campaigns.

## The Operating System Model

Traditional software manages files.

Saga Atlas manages stories.

Everything revolves around a living campaign model that understands
context, history, intent, and relationships.

## Campaign Memory

The application should remember:

-   every important decision
-   recurring NPCs
-   unresolved mysteries
-   recurring locations
-   promises made
-   debts owed
-   changing relationships
-   evolving factions
-   historical events

Memory should improve recommendations rather than becoming passive
history.

## Story Awareness

Future versions should recognize patterns such as:

-   neglected story threads
-   overused encounter types
-   forgotten NPCs
-   unresolved promises
-   repeated locations
-   pacing imbalance
-   excessive combat
-   lack of discovery

The Co‑Pilot recommends corrections without enforcing them.

## Living Campaign

Campaigns should feel alive.

Time progresses.

Factions pursue goals.

Colonies evolve.

Resources fluctuate.

Weather changes.

News spreads.

NPCs remember previous encounters.

The world advances even when the players do not.

## Invisible Intelligence

The most successful implementation is one where the GM notices better
stories but rarely notices the software itself.

## Future Capabilities

Potential future modules include:

-   Dynamic economy simulation
-   Procedural colony growth
-   Strategic faction AI
-   Living star maps
-   Trade route optimization
-   Expedition planning
-   Collaborative online campaigns
-   Voice-assisted play
-   AI-assisted note summarization
-   Automatic campaign timelines
-   Interactive rulebook indexing

Each capability should integrate with the existing architecture rather
than introducing competing workflows.

## Final Architectural Principle

Whenever uncertainty exists, ask:

"Which solution helps the Game Master tell a better story with less
effort?"

That answer should guide every future design decision.

## Closing Statement

Saga Atlas should become the software that disappears.

The Game Master remembers the story.

The players remember the adventure.

No one remembers the software---and that is the measure of its success.
