# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 18 --- Campaign Director, Trackers & Narrative State

## Purpose

The Campaign Director maintains the living state of the campaign and
continuously informs recommendations without replacing the Game Master's
authority.

## Philosophy

Track the campaign, not the mechanics.

Trackers exist to influence story direction rather than simulate every
rule.

## Narrative Trackers

Core campaign trackers:

-   Danger
-   Hope
-   Heat
-   Resources
-   Momentum
-   Mystery
-   Pressure
-   Exposure
-   Reputation
-   Morale

These trackers are narrative influences rather than hard game
statistics.

## Scene Trackers

Scene-specific trackers include:

-   Time Remaining
-   Countdown
-   Alarm Level
-   Structural Integrity
-   Oxygen
-   Power
-   Infection
-   Pursuit
-   Discovery Progress

Scene trackers automatically retire when the scene ends unless promoted
to campaign state.

## Track Types

Each tracker supports:

-   Current value
-   Minimum / Maximum
-   Color band
-   Threshold events
-   Related entities
-   Related threads
-   Story consequences

## Threshold Events

Crossing thresholds may trigger recommendations.

Example

Danger reaches 80%

↓

Suggested consequence:

"Security mobilizes."

No automatic story changes occur until accepted.

## Timers

Timers represent future events.

Examples

-   Rescue Shuttle Arrival
-   Reactor Failure
-   Storm Front
-   Pirate Ambush
-   Corporate Audit

Timers may be:

-   Paused
-   Advanced
-   Completed
-   Cancelled

## Conditions

Conditions apply to:

-   Characters
-   Groups
-   Locations
-   Assets
-   Missions

Examples

-   Hidden
-   Injured
-   Damaged
-   Infected
-   Alerted
-   Compromised
-   Low Supplies

Conditions influence Story Engine recommendations.

## Director State

The Director State summarizes:

-   Active threads
-   Current pressure
-   Narrative trackers
-   Open timers
-   Campaign momentum
-   Current recommendations

It replaces numerous legacy dashboard panels.

## Acceptance Criteria

-   Trackers support story rather than bookkeeping.
-   Thresholds create recommendations instead of mandatory events.
-   Director State provides a concise overview of campaign health.
-   Narrative state remains visible without overwhelming the workspace.
