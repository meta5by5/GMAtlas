# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 50 --- The Saga Atlas Constitution

## Purpose

This concluding document distills the entire Design Constitution into a
concise set of enduring constitutional articles that should govern every
future decision.

## Article I --- The Campaign

The campaign is the primary product.

Everything else exists to help understand, evolve, and experience it.

## Article II --- The Game Master

The Game Master always retains creative authority.

Saga Atlas advises.

It never dictates.

## Article III --- Story

Story is permanent.

Mechanics are interchangeable.

Rules systems are lenses through which the story is experienced.

## Article IV --- Context

Context is the foundation of every recommendation, workflow, and
interface.

The application should infer before it asks.

## Article V --- Mission Control

Mission Control is the primary workspace.

Editing, reference material, and supporting tools remain secondary.

## Article VI --- Relationships

Relationships create meaning.

The Context Graph is the living model of the campaign.

## Article VII --- Knowledge

Every important concept should be connected.

Knowledge should be discoverable through relationships rather than
folders.

## Article VIII --- Persistence

Campaign data is sacred.

No release should jeopardize campaign continuity.

## Article IX --- Extensibility

Future capabilities extend the platform through engines, templates,
activities, and plugins.

Core architectural principles remain unchanged.

## Article X --- Frictionless Empowerment

Every design decision should reduce cognitive effort while increasing
meaningful creative control.

## Constitutional Test

Before accepting any feature, ask:

-   Does it help tell better stories?
-   Does it reduce friction?
-   Does it strengthen context?
-   Does it preserve continuity?
-   Does it fit the architecture?

If the answer is no, redesign.

## Closing Declaration

Saga Atlas is not merely software.

It is a Campaign Operating System whose purpose is to help Game Masters
create richer stories, preserve living worlds, and spend their attention
where it matters most:

At the table.
