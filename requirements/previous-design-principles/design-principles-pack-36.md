# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 36 --- Scenario Engine, Adventure Templates & Guided Play

## Purpose

The Scenario Engine provides structured adventures that remain flexible
enough for emergent storytelling.

It bridges authored scenarios, procedural generation, and campaign
continuity.

## Design Philosophy

The engine proposes situations, not scripts.

Every scenario consists of:

-   Premise
-   Objectives
-   Actors
-   Locations
-   Opportunities
-   Complications
-   Escalation Paths
-   Resolution Conditions

## Scenario Templates

Supported templates include:

-   Investigation
-   Frontier Exploration
-   Colony Crisis
-   Trade Mission
-   Search and Rescue
-   Military Operation
-   First Contact
-   Salvage
-   Diplomacy
-   Survival Horror

Templates describe structure rather than content.

## Guided Play

The Scenario Engine should guide the GM by answering:

-   What is happening now?
-   What could happen next?
-   What are the likely consequences?
-   Which entities are involved?
-   Which rules lens best supports this activity?

## Dynamic Beats

Each scenario progresses through story beats.

Typical beat types:

-   Introduction
-   Discovery
-   Escalation
-   Complication
-   Choice
-   Climax
-   Resolution
-   Aftermath

The Story Engine may skip, repeat, or merge beats based on campaign
context.

## Reusable Components

Templates should reuse:

-   Entity Templates
-   Activity Engine
-   Oracle Engine
-   Context Graph
-   Campaign Director
-   Mission Control

No scenario should duplicate core logic.

## Acceptance Criteria

-   Scenarios adapt to existing campaigns.
-   Templates remain system-neutral.
-   Guided play reduces preparation time.
-   Emergent stories remain possible.
