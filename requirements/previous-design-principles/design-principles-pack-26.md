# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 26 --- Future Vision, Design Heuristics & Product Evolution

## Purpose

This document records the long-term vision and heuristics that guide
future decisions after the core architecture is complete.

## Design Heuristics

When multiple solutions are possible, prefer the one that:

1.  Reduces cognitive load.
2.  Preserves campaign continuity.
3.  Increases story awareness.
4.  Minimizes navigation.
5.  Encourages discovery.
6.  Requires fewer clicks.
7.  Avoids duplicate data.
8.  Strengthens relationships.
9.  Keeps the GM in control.
10. Leaves the workspace cleaner than before.

## Campaign Operating System

The campaign---not a file or database---is the primary object.

Everything else exists to understand, present, and evolve the campaign.

## Invisible Software

The best Saga Atlas session feels as though the application disappears.

The GM thinks about: - story - characters - mysteries - choices

not windows, tabs, forms, or menus.

## Living Workspace

Mission Control should eventually become a living dashboard that
continuously adapts to:

-   campaign phase
-   current activity
-   available time
-   player count
-   preferred rules lens

without requiring manual configuration.

## AI Philosophy

Future AI capabilities should:

-   explain recommendations
-   cite campaign context
-   cite rule references
-   identify inconsistencies
-   summarize progress
-   never overwrite campaign history

## Evolution Rules

Future features must integrate with:

-   Storage Kernel
-   Context Graph
-   Story Engine
-   Activity Engine
-   Mission Control

Creating isolated subsystems is discouraged.

## Success Metrics

Saga Atlas succeeds when:

-   preparation time decreases
-   session interruptions decrease
-   continuity improves
-   forgotten threads decrease
-   reference lookups become faster
-   campaigns become easier to resume after weeks away

## Acceptance Criteria

Every major release should make the application:

-   simpler to understand
-   more context-aware
-   more extensible
-   more reliable
-   more enjoyable to use

The philosophy of Frictionless Empowerment remains the governing
principle for all future development.
