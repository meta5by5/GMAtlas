# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 13 --- Story Engine, Consequences & Campaign Intelligence

## Purpose

The Story Engine advances the campaign without taking narrative control
away from the Game Master.

Its role is to observe, connect, recommend, and remember.

## Core Principle

The Story Engine proposes.

The Game Master decides.

Nothing becomes canon until accepted.

## Campaign Intelligence Engine (CIE)

The CIE continuously evaluates:

-   Current mission
-   Active threads
-   Entity relationships
-   Current location
-   Activity
-   Campaign trackers
-   Story momentum
-   Time progression

The result is a prioritized set of recommendations.

## Recommendation Types

-   Continue Story
-   What Happens Next?
-   What Did I Overlook?
-   Suggested Oracle
-   Suggested NPC
-   Suggested Location
-   Suggested Complication
-   Suggested Reward
-   Suggested Consequence

Each recommendation includes:

-   Confidence %
-   Why it was suggested
-   Related entities
-   Related threads

## Consequence Model

Every accepted Story Action may generate future consequences.

States:

-   Dormant
-   Building
-   Active
-   Escalated
-   Resolved

Consequences influence future scenes rather than immediately
interrupting the story.

## Time Progression

Instead of generating scenes, Saga Atlas advances campaign time.

Supported increments:

-   Minutes
-   Hours
-   Day
-   Week
-   Month

Time advancement updates:

-   Threads
-   NPC schedules
-   Resources
-   Heat
-   Danger
-   Hope
-   Faction activity

## What Did I Overlook?

The Story Engine should periodically search for:

-   Forgotten NPCs
-   Unresolved clues
-   Idle factions
-   Unused assets
-   Expiring timers
-   Missing consequences

The result is a concise reminder list.

## Story Momentum

Momentum reflects narrative pacing.

Levels:

-   Recovering
-   Stable
-   Building
-   Escalating
-   Critical

Momentum influences recommendation weighting.

## Acceptance Criteria

-   The Story Engine never dictates outcomes.
-   Recommendations are contextual and explainable.
-   Campaign continuity improves over time.
-   Every accepted recommendation updates the Context Graph.
