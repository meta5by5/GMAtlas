# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 77 --- Story Thread Lifecycle & Narrative Resolution

## Purpose

This document defines how story threads are created, evolve, intersect,
and conclude throughout a campaign.

## Philosophy

Threads are the smallest persistent units of narrative continuity.

A campaign is the interaction of many threads rather than a single
linear plot.

## Thread States

-   Seeded
-   Active
-   Escalating
-   Dormant
-   Converging
-   Resolved
-   Archived

Threads should move naturally between these states based on campaign
events.

## Thread Attributes

Every thread records:

-   Summary
-   Origin
-   Related entities
-   Related locations
-   Related factions
-   Priority
-   Urgency
-   Momentum
-   Last activity
-   Possible consequences

## Thread Convergence

Multiple threads may converge into:

-   A revelation
-   A conflict
-   A major discovery
-   A campaign milestone

The Story Engine should recognize opportunities for convergence without
forcing them.

## Dormant Threads

Dormant threads should occasionally reappear when context becomes
relevant again.

Dormancy is not abandonment.

## Resolution

Resolving a thread should:

-   Update campaign memory
-   Affect relationships
-   Trigger consequences where appropriate
-   Create historical references

## Acceptance Criteria

-   Thread management strengthens long-term continuity.
-   Recommendations help revive forgotten threads.
-   Resolution creates meaningful campaign history rather than simply
    closing tasks.
