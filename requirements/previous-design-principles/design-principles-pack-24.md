# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 24 --- Multi-System Gameplay Framework & Activity Engine

## Purpose

Saga Atlas is designed to allow a single campaign to seamlessly
transition between multiple tabletop RPG systems without disrupting the
narrative.

The Activity Engine determines *what the players are doing*, while Rules
Lenses determine *how the activity is resolved*.

## Core Principle

Story remains continuous.

Mechanics are interchangeable.

The campaign never changes because the rules system changes.

## Activities

Activities describe intent rather than mechanics.

Examples:

-   Conversation
-   Exploration
-   Investigation
-   Negotiation
-   Travel
-   Combat
-   Colony Management
-   Salvage
-   Trade
-   Crafting
-   Research
-   Medical
-   Survival
-   Downtime
-   Social Encounter

Activities drive recommendations.

## Rules Lens Mapping

An activity may support multiple systems.

Example

Exploration

→ Starforged → Traveller → Hostile

Combat

→ Five Parsecs From Home → Hostile → Traveller

Settlement

→ Planetfall → Homebrew Colony

The GM always has final choice.

## Transition Rules

Changing the Rules Lens should preserve:

-   Story context
-   Active entities
-   Threads
-   Objectives
-   Locations
-   Relationships
-   Journal history

Only the mechanical presentation changes.

## Character Translation

Each entity may contain multiple stat templates.

Example

Character

Starforged Stats

Five Parsecs Stats

Traveller Stats

The active template is determined by the current Rules Lens.

## Activity Recommendations

The Activity Engine recommends:

-   Rules Lens
-   Dice Method
-   Relevant PDFs
-   Oracle Categories
-   Story Actions

Recommendations are based on context rather than user configuration.

## Session Flow

Mission Control

↓

Current Activity

↓

Recommended Lens

↓

Gameplay

↓

Outcome

↓

Context Update

The story remains the center of the experience.

## Acceptance Criteria

-   Switching systems requires no campaign conversion.
-   Activities remain system-neutral.
-   Multiple stat templates coexist for the same entity.
-   The Activity Engine reduces decision fatigue while preserving
    flexibility.
