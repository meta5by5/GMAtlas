# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 12 --- Journal, Session Log & Co‑Pilot Conversation

## Purpose

The Journal is not merely a log.

It is the living conversation between the Game Master and the Campaign
Operating System.

The Session Log captures what happened.

The Journal captures what it means.

## Design Philosophy

Traditional journals are historical.

Saga Atlas journals are predictive.

Entries influence future recommendations through the Context Graph.

## Journal Types

-   Narrative
-   GM Note
-   Player Decision
-   NPC Observation
-   Clue
-   Rumor
-   Discovery
-   Consequence
-   Idea
-   Session Summary

Each entry is an entity.

## Conversation Model

The Co‑Pilot should respond to journal entries.

Example

GM: "I think the administrator is hiding something."

Co‑Pilot: Evidence supports this theory. The maintenance chief also had
opportunity.

Suggestions remain optional.

## Acceptance Workflow

Every recommendation supports:

-   Accept
-   Ignore
-   Modify
-   Pin for Later

Accepted recommendations become campaign history.

Ignored recommendations disappear without affecting the story.

## Linked Context

Journal entries may link to:

-   Entities
-   Groups
-   Locations
-   Missions
-   Threads
-   PDFs
-   Dice Rolls
-   Oracle Results

Links are bidirectional.

## Dice Integration

Every roll may be appended automatically.

Recorded information includes:

-   Rules Lens
-   Roll Formula
-   Result
-   Outcome
-   Related Entity
-   Related Scene

## Timeline

Journal entries build the campaign timeline automatically.

Campaign

↓

Act

↓

Mission

↓

Scene

↓

Beat

↓

Journal Entry

Selecting any level filters the journal.

## Search

Journal search spans:

-   Full text
-   Tags
-   Linked entities
-   Linked PDFs
-   Threads
-   Story actions

## Drawer Behavior

The Journal opens in a resizable drawer.

Mission Control remains visible.

No full-page navigation.

## Session Summaries

At session end the Co‑Pilot proposes:

-   Major events
-   Outstanding threads
-   Changed relationships
-   New entities
-   Future hooks

The GM edits before saving.

## Acceptance Criteria

-   Journal never interrupts play.
-   Conversation remains contextual.
-   Every entry strengthens campaign memory.
-   Journal supports future story recommendations rather than acting
    solely as an archive.
