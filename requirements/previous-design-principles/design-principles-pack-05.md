# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 05 --- WHAT Workspace, Story Context & Story Shifts

## Purpose

The WHAT workspace answers one question:

**What is happening right now?**

Unlike traditional scene generators, this workspace represents the
living state of the current story beat.

## Story Context

The current situation is defined by:

-   Current Event
-   Immediate Problem
-   Current Opportunity
-   Current Conflict
-   Current Discovery
-   Current Surprise
-   Story Momentum

These values describe the scene, not the mechanics.

## Story Actions

The Story Actions panel is the primary interaction point.

Actions include:

-   Reveal Clue
-   Introduce NPC
-   Add Encounter
-   Escalate Situation
-   Reduce Pressure
-   Reward Players
-   Introduce Complication
-   Advance Time
-   Resolve Event
-   Shift Story

## Shift Story

Shift Story is the GM's primary steering control.

Rather than editing dozens of fields, the GM chooses a narrative intent.

Examples

Travel

Escalate

Recover

Reveal

Delay

Investigate

Negotiate

Explore

Recover Resources

Raise Stakes

The Story Engine updates the Context Graph and Story Context to support
the chosen direction.

## Co-Pilot Integration

The Co-Pilot may recommend

"If nothing changes..."

Potential consequences

Missed opportunities

Likely NPC reactions

Suggested oracle

Suggested PDF reference

These remain suggestions.

The GM decides.

## Scene Inspiration

Scene Inspiration belongs under the current situation.

It contains

-   Narrative draft
-   Suggested sensory details
-   Environmental flavor
-   NPC motivations
-   Optional twists

The GM may copy, edit, or ignore these.

## Acceptance Criteria

-   The workspace communicates the scene in under five seconds.
-   Story Actions are faster than editing fields.
-   Story Shifts update context rather than replacing story history.
-   The Co-Pilot never forces a narrative outcome.
