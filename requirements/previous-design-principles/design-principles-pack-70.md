# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 70 --- Final Vision: The Campaign Companion

## Purpose

This volume describes the experiential goal of Saga Atlas after all
architectural objectives have been achieved.

## The Experience

The Game Master opens Saga Atlas and immediately sees:

-   What is happening.
-   Who is involved.
-   Why it matters.
-   What is most likely needed next.

No searching.

No setup.

No remembering.

## Invisible Assistance

Saga Atlas quietly:

-   remembers campaign history,
-   tracks evolving relationships,
-   surfaces forgotten threads,
-   links rules and references,
-   recommends relevant tools,
-   preserves continuity.

The GM remains the storyteller.

## Design Promise

Every interaction should satisfy at least one of these goals:

-   Reduce friction.
-   Increase context.
-   Preserve continuity.
-   Encourage creativity.
-   Build confidence.

## The Ideal Session

The GM spends nearly all of the session:

-   making story decisions,
-   interacting with players,
-   interpreting outcomes.

The software performs the supporting work.

## Enduring Principle

Technology exists to amplify imagination---not replace it.

## Final Statement

Saga Atlas should become the trusted companion that remembers
everything, organizes everything, and quietly helps Game Masters create
unforgettable campaigns while never taking ownership of the story
itself.
