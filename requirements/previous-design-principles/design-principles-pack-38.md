# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 38 --- Living World Simulation & Autonomous World State

## Purpose

The Living World Engine allows the campaign world to evolve
independently of the player characters while remaining understandable
and controllable by the GM.

## Philosophy

The world should feel alive.

Events occur because organizations, people, environments, and time
continue to act---even when the players are elsewhere.

The GM remains free to accept, modify, or ignore any proposed
development.

## Simulation Domains

The Living World Engine monitors:

-   Factions
-   Colonies
-   Economies
-   Infrastructure
-   Politics
-   Ecology
-   Exploration
-   Trade
-   Security
-   Scientific progress

Each domain contributes recommendations rather than deterministic
outcomes.

## Background Turns

World progression occurs through configurable background turns.

Examples:

-   Every Scene
-   Every Day
-   Every Week
-   Every Campaign Milestone

Background turns update narrative state rather than attempting full
simulation.

## Faction Agendas

Every faction should maintain:

-   Long-term goals
-   Current objective
-   Available resources
-   Territory
-   Relationships
-   Pressure
-   Recent actions

The engine proposes logical next moves based on these attributes.

## World Events

Potential events include:

-   Settlement growth
-   Supply shortages
-   Pirate raids
-   Scientific breakthroughs
-   Diplomatic incidents
-   Environmental disasters
-   Alien discoveries
-   Corporate initiatives

Events become story opportunities only after GM approval.

## Player Impact

Player actions influence future world recommendations through:

-   Reputation
-   Resource changes
-   Relationship shifts
-   Territory control
-   Public knowledge
-   Faction pressure

## Design Rules

-   Background activity must remain explainable.
-   Simulations should emphasize story relevance over realism.
-   The engine should avoid overwhelming the GM with notifications.
-   Significant events should accumulate into meaningful campaign
    history.

## Acceptance Criteria

-   The world evolves naturally between sessions.
-   Recommendations remain concise and contextual.
-   Campaign continuity is strengthened without increasing bookkeeping.
-   The GM always retains final authority over world changes.
