# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 65 --- Design Constitution Maintenance Guide

## Purpose

This guide defines how the Design Constitution itself should evolve as
Saga Atlas grows.

## Living Constitution

The Constitution is a living architectural reference.

It should evolve through additions and clarifications rather than
wholesale rewrites.

Historical intent should remain traceable.

## Amendment Process

Every amendment should include:

-   Motivation
-   Related Design Packs
-   Affected Engines
-   Affected Workflows
-   Compatibility Notes
-   Acceptance Criteria

Major architectural changes should also create an Architectural Decision
Record (ADR).

## Review Cadence

Review the Constitution:

-   Before major releases
-   After significant refactors
-   When introducing a new engine
-   When adding a new extension point
-   After major gameplay usability studies

## Consistency Checks

Verify that:

-   Terminology remains consistent.
-   Mission Control remains the primary workflow.
-   No new duplicate sources of truth have emerged.
-   Frictionless Empowerment remains the guiding UX principle.
-   Documentation reflects the current implementation.

## Deprecation Policy

Older guidance should be marked as:

-   Active
-   Deprecated
-   Superseded

Avoid deleting historical rationale.

## Stewardship

Every contributor is responsible for protecting:

-   Campaign continuity
-   Context-first workflows
-   Architectural simplicity
-   Long-term maintainability
-   User trust

## Closing Principle

The Constitution should remain the stable reference that explains not
only how Saga Atlas is built, but why it is built that way.
