# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

## Pack 01 --- Product Vision & Design Principles

### Mission Statement

Saga Atlas is a **Campaign Operating System**.

It is not merely a campaign manager, scene generator, or AI Game Master.
Its purpose is to reduce cognitive load while preserving complete
creative control for the Game Master.

## Frictionless Empowerment

The primary design philosophy is:

> Remove unnecessary decisions while increasing meaningful decisions.

### Core Principles

-   The story is the product.
-   Rules are interchangeable lenses.
-   The GM always remains the author.
-   Suggestions never become decisions.
-   Every click should either increase understanding or advance the
    story.
-   Navigation follows the GM's thinking, not the application's data
    structures.
-   Mission Control is the application; editors and libraries become
    supporting tools.
-   Progressive disclosure keeps only the current decision visible.
-   Persistence is sacred: software updates must never destroy campaign
    data.

## One Question, One Workspace

The interface revolves around five questions:

-   **WHO** --- Who is involved?
-   **WHERE** --- Where is this happening?
-   **WHAT** --- What is happening?
-   **WHY** --- Why does it matter?
-   **HOW** --- How is the story being experienced?

The workspace adapts to the active question rather than navigating to
different pages.

## Three Visual Tiers

### Primary

Current context, current decision, current recommendation.

### Secondary

Story actions, campaign state, Co‑Pilot.

### Background

Reference material, settings, journals, entity libraries, documents.

## Product Goal

A GM should be able to open Saga Atlas and begin playing within thirty
seconds.

The software should disappear into the background so only the story
remains.
