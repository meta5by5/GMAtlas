# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 74 --- Narrative Design Patterns & Story Structure

## Purpose

This document defines reusable narrative patterns that Saga Atlas can
recognize and support without constraining creativity.

## Philosophy

Stories are composed of recurring patterns rather than rigid plots.

Saga Atlas should recognize these structures and assist the Game Master
in managing them.

## Core Narrative Patterns

### Mystery

Characteristics:

-   Unknown truth
-   Clues
-   False leads
-   Revelation

Recommended Story Actions:

-   Reveal Clue
-   Investigate
-   Question NPC
-   Connect Evidence

### Exploration

Characteristics:

-   Discovery
-   Navigation
-   Risk
-   Reward

Recommended Story Actions:

-   Survey
-   Travel
-   Discover
-   Document

### Conflict

Characteristics:

-   Opposing goals
-   Escalation
-   Resolution

Recommended Story Actions:

-   Negotiate
-   Fight
-   Retreat
-   Escalate

### Survival

Characteristics:

-   Scarcity
-   Time pressure
-   Resource management

Recommended Story Actions:

-   Consume Resources
-   Repair
-   Rest
-   Search

## Story Pattern Recognition

The Story Engine should identify emerging structures from campaign
state.

Examples:

-   Too many unresolved clues → Suggest revelation.
-   Long travel sequence → Suggest discovery.
-   Rising danger → Suggest complication.
-   Multiple allied factions → Suggest political opportunity.

## Pattern Composition

Complex campaigns combine patterns.

Example:

Exploration → Mystery → Conflict → Survival

Saga Atlas should allow multiple active patterns simultaneously.

## Acceptance Criteria

-   Story patterns remain advisory.
-   Patterns improve recommendations without scripting outcomes.
-   Narrative structures emerge from campaign context rather than fixed
    templates.
