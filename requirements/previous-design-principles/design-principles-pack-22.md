# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 22 --- Campaign Operating Workflow & Story Lifecycle

## Purpose

This document defines the canonical lifecycle of a Saga Atlas campaign.

The campaign should flow naturally between preparation, play,
reflection, and progression while minimizing bookkeeping.

## Campaign Lifecycle

The campaign proceeds through repeating cycles:

1.  Orient
2.  Decide
3.  Experience
4.  Resolve
5.  Record
6.  Advance

Every feature in Saga Atlas supports one or more of these stages.

## 1. Orient

Mission Control presents:

-   Current Story
-   Current Party
-   Current Location
-   Active Threads
-   Current Objective
-   Current Recommendation

The GM immediately understands the state of play.

## 2. Decide

The GM selects a Story Action.

Examples:

-   Continue Story
-   Shift Story
-   Travel
-   Investigate
-   Negotiate
-   Explore
-   Fight
-   Build
-   Trade

The Story Engine updates recommendations before mechanics are selected.

## 3. Experience

The HOW workspace recommends:

-   Rules Lens
-   Dice Method
-   Oracles
-   PDFs
-   Relevant Entities

Gameplay occurs without leaving Mission Control.

## 4. Resolve

Outcomes update:

-   Context Graph
-   Narrative Trackers
-   Threads
-   Entity Relationships
-   Campaign Memory

Only accepted outcomes become canonical.

## 5. Record

The Journal proposes:

-   Session Notes
-   Consequences
-   New Discoveries
-   New Relationships
-   New Entities

The GM edits before saving.

## 6. Advance

End Scene performs:

-   Advance Time
-   Update Timers
-   Update Momentum
-   Refresh Recommendations
-   Save Campaign

The next scene begins with updated context.

## Story Continuity

Campaign continuity is preserved by:

-   Context Graph
-   Campaign Memory
-   Relationship Engine
-   Narrative Trackers
-   Journal Links

The story remains consistent even when rules systems change.

## Acceptance Criteria

-   Every session follows the same intuitive loop.
-   Mission Control always reflects the current campaign state.
-   Story progression remains independent of mechanics.
-   Campaign continuity improves naturally over time.
