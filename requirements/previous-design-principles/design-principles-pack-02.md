# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 02 --- Campaign Operating System & Mission Control

## Campaign Operating System

Saga Atlas is organized around a single concept: the campaign is the
primary object. Every subsystem---entities, journals, rules, oracles,
templates, trackers, and PDFs---exists to support the campaign rather
than competing for attention.

## Mission Control

Mission Control is the application home screen.

### Objectives

-   Orient the GM in under 5 seconds.
-   Surface only the current decision.
-   Preserve campaign continuity.
-   Make story progression frictionless.

## Layout

Top Bar - Saga Atlas title - Current Campaign - Continue Story - What
Happens Next

Context Strip - WHO - WHERE - WHAT - WHY - HOW

Breadcrumb Timeline Campaign → Act → Mission → Scene → Beat → Moment

Adaptive Workspace Changes according to the active context card.

Persistent Co‑Pilot Always visible on the right.

Drawers - Journal - Oracle - Entity Inspector - Relationship Graph -
Settings

## Context Cards

WHO Current group, party, nearby NPCs, factions, assets.

WHERE Planet, region, district, encounter site, hazards.

WHAT Situation, discovery, encounter, complication.

WHY Objective, thread, stakes, consequences.

HOW Recommended activity and rules lens: - Starforged - Five Parsecs
From Home - Hostile - Traveller

## Story Actions

Every workspace provides actions before editing.

Examples

WHO - Add NPC - Link Asset - Assign Mission

WHERE - Travel - Discover Location - Enter Structure

WHAT - Reveal Clue - Add Encounter - Advance Time

WHY - Increase Stakes - Complete Objective - Advance Thread

HOW - Conversation - Exploration - Combat - Trade - Colony

## Design Rules

-   Never navigate away from Mission Control during play.
-   Editing occurs inline or in drawers.
-   Libraries are reference tools, not primary navigation.
-   Every workspace should fit on one screen when practical.
-   Co‑Pilot assists but never overrides the GM.
