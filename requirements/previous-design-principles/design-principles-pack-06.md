# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 06 --- WHY Workspace, Campaign Continuity & Threads

## Purpose

The WHY workspace answers one question:

**Why does this scene matter?**

It provides the narrative reason behind the current situation and
connects each scene to the broader campaign.

## Core Elements

The WHY workspace is centered on five concepts:

-   Objective
-   Stakes
-   Opportunity
-   Thread
-   Consequence

These elements should always explain why the current scene exists.

## Mission Structure

Campaign → Act → Mission → Objective → Scene → Beat

Objectives exist independently of scenes. Scenes serve objectives.

## Threads

Threads are persistent story questions.

Examples:

-   What happened to the survey team?
-   Who sabotaged the reactor?
-   Why are the pirates unusually organized?

Each thread tracks:

-   Status
-   Priority
-   Related entities
-   Related locations
-   Related missions
-   Last advanced scene

## Stakes

Stakes answer:

"What becomes worse if nothing changes?"

Stakes should affect campaign trackers such as:

-   Danger
-   Hope
-   Heat
-   Resources
-   Time
-   Reputation

## Opportunities

Every meaningful scene should contain at least one opportunity.

Examples:

-   New employment
-   New ally
-   Salvage
-   Hidden route
-   Political leverage
-   Scientific discovery

## Consequence Engine

Every accepted Story Action may create one or more future consequences.

Consequences are recorded rather than immediately resolved.

Possible states:

-   Pending
-   Escalating
-   Active
-   Resolved
-   Forgotten

## Story Momentum

Momentum reflects campaign pacing rather than player success.

Values:

-   Recovering
-   Stable
-   Building
-   Escalating
-   Critical

Momentum influences future recommendations.

## Campaign Memory

Mission Control remembers:

-   unresolved mysteries
-   recurring NPCs
-   faction relationships
-   previous locations
-   prior consequences

These are surfaced automatically as context.

## Acceptance Criteria

-   Every scene links to at least one objective.
-   Every objective links to one or more threads.
-   Consequences remain visible until resolved.
-   The WHY workspace explains why the current scene deserves attention.
