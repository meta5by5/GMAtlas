# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 75 --- Emergent Gameplay & Player Agency

## Purpose

This document defines how Saga Atlas should encourage emergent gameplay
while preserving complete player and Game Master agency.

## Philosophy

The most memorable campaigns arise from meaningful choices interacting
with persistent consequences.

Saga Atlas should amplify those interactions rather than predetermine
them.

## Principles

### Agency First

Players should always have multiple viable approaches.

Recommendations expand options rather than narrow them.

### Consequences Persist

Choices create lasting effects through:

-   Relationships
-   Reputation
-   Resources
-   World state
-   Future opportunities

### Surprise Through Context

Unexpected developments should emerge naturally from existing campaign
state instead of arbitrary randomness.

### Opportunity Over Prescription

Recommendations should invite exploration.

Examples:

-   "This faction may seek revenge."
-   "The abandoned relay could contain valuable data."

Rather than:

-   "The next scene is..."

## Emergent Systems

Support interaction among:

-   Factions
-   NPC agendas
-   Environmental hazards
-   Timers
-   Discoveries
-   Resources
-   Story threads

Interesting stories emerge from their interaction.

## Measuring Agency

Healthy campaigns demonstrate:

-   Multiple meaningful decisions
-   Recoverable failures
-   Lasting consequences
-   Unexpected opportunities
-   Diverse solutions

## Acceptance Criteria

-   Saga Atlas increases player agency.
-   Emergent stories become more common as campaign history grows.
-   Recommendations support creativity without reducing freedom.
