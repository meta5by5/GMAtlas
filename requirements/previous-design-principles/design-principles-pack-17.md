# Saga Atlas Design Constitution

# Pack 17 --- Workflow Model, GM Experience & Frictionless Empowerment

## Purpose

This document defines the intended gameplay workflow.

Saga Atlas is designed around how a Game Master thinks during play
rather than around software features.

## Frictionless Empowerment

Definition:

Increase agency while decreasing effort.

The application should continuously reduce bookkeeping, navigation and
repetitive decision making while preserving complete creative ownership.

## Gameplay Loop

Observe Context

↓

Understand Story

↓

Choose Story Action

↓

Apply Rules Lens

↓

Resolve Outcome

↓

Update Context

↓

Continue Story

This loop repeats throughout the session.

## Session Startup

Opening a campaign should immediately present:

-   Current Mission
-   Current Party
-   Current Location
-   Current Objective
-   Current Recommendation

The GM should not need to navigate before beginning play.

## Pause & Adjust

Whenever desired, the GM may switch to editing mode.

Editing updates context rather than interrupting gameplay.

Examples:

-   Change location
-   Add NPC
-   Raise danger
-   Complete objective
-   Introduce new thread

## Continue Story

The primary action of Saga Atlas.

Rather than generating random scenes, Continue Story advances the
existing campaign.

Inputs:

-   Context Graph
-   Story Engine
-   Current Activity
-   Time
-   Threads
-   Consequences

Outputs:

-   Updated story context
-   Suggested actions
-   New opportunities
-   Consequences
-   Co‑Pilot observations

## Shift Story

The GM may intentionally steer the narrative.

Story shifts include:

-   Escalate
-   Recover
-   Explore
-   Negotiate
-   Reveal
-   Delay
-   Travel
-   Reward
-   Investigate

The Story Engine adapts recommendations without rewriting history.

## End Scene

Ending a scene performs:

-   Advance timeline
-   Resolve accepted actions
-   Update momentum
-   Refresh campaign memory
-   Prompt session notes
-   Preserve continuity

## Design Rules

-   Story always remains visible.
-   Editing never hides context.
-   Rules never replace narrative.
-   Every interaction strengthens campaign continuity.

## Acceptance Criteria

-   A new GM can begin play within thirty seconds.
-   Experienced GMs rarely leave Mission Control.
-   The software feels like a creative partner rather than a database.
